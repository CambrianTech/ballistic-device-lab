GPU ACTIVE-LEARNING GEOMETRY SEARCH -- completed 2026-10-22

Outcome
Across three independent search repeats, learned proposals beat equal-budget
uniform random search on fresh-seed evaluation of frozen winners.
Mean steering-response score: learned .354063, random .312915, previous
triangle .226597. Learned-minus-random in the three repeats:
.0291214, .0321741, .0621490 (2.91, 3.22, 6.21 percentage points).
These scores are probabilities, NOT efficiencies or predicted DC voltages.

Work completed
2,724 geometry evaluations including repeats and diagnostic sets; 119,537,664
ray trajectories in the saved learning dataset. CPU/GPU validation is additional.
All samples retained in learning-data.jsonl, including poor geometries. No
RANSAC rejection, reinforcement learning, or unreported discarded samples.
This is a surrogate-assisted search over four continuous geometry variables.

65,536 specular test rays spanning four geometries and four terminals:

Diffuse/specular mixture comparisons agreed within .0032 exit probability.

Numerical incident and repair
One fresh holdout sample exceeded the original 10,000-reflection cap. The
evaluator stopped with an error, never scored or dropped the ray. The cap was
raised to 100,000 in BOTH CPU and GPU implementations. Frozen geometry files
were preserved, and only the missing holdout evaluation was resumed. Independent
CPU/GPU replay of that seed and those geometries passed. Rare long trajectories
also emphasize that omitted bulk scattering remains a physical limitation.
Elapsed run time is null in the final JSON because the resumed run spans an
interruption; no fabricated combined compute-time figure is reported.

Objective and search
Same scale-free square/contact setup as METHODS.txt. Triangle width [.25,1.5],
height [.25,1.1], vertical center [-.35,.35], tip skew [-.6,.6] times half-width.
All triangles lie within the cavity and clear contact openings.
Score averages the change in bottom-minus-top exit probability between the
cosine input and imposed +/-20.5 degree collimation, at wall specularity 1 and .8.
Four thousand ninety-six rays per terminal/condition; eight conditions per score.
192 pilot training and 128 pilot validation geometries. Predictive gate R2>.25;
observed .40764, mean absolute error .03807. Benchmark gate GPU speedup>2 passed.
Pilot data were not given to either benchmark arm; only used for go/no-go and
feature diagnostics. This shared setup cost is additional to the arm budgets.

Each of three repeats: 64 shared initial geometries, followed by five rounds
of 64 new geometries per arm, 384 evaluations per arm. Random arm samples the
same bounds uniformly. Learned arm fits 128 bootstrapped ExtraTrees with leaf
minimum 2. Proposal pool: 4096 global + 4096 local perturbations around eight
high scorers. Acquire 48 using mean+.75*tree-standard-deviation, with spatial
diversity filters, and 16 fresh random explorations. Ensemble dispersion is a
heuristic exploration signal, not a calibrated confidence interval.
Same simulation seed per round for both arms. Winners selected using training
scores only and written to frozen-winners-N.json before holdout. Four new seeds,
65536 rays per condition, validate each winner and the previous triangle.
All three repeats beat random; three repeats are not a general proof of superiority.
The advantage is for this acquisition method and geometry range, not all searches.

Reduced-set finding
Pilot permutation importance ranked width, height, offset, skew in that order.
Dropping offset/skew reduced pilot-validation R2 from .408 to .148. Because that
set also selected features, an additional independent 256-geometry test was run:
four-variable R2=.35340; width/height-only R2=.11957. Thus no variables were
removed. Strongest predictor does not mean sufficient predictor. No RANSAC
filter is warranted merely because some shapes are unusual.

Limits / next useful modeling step
Only classical geometric sensitivity is learned. The injection distribution is
imposed; it is not derived from drive voltage or current. No self-consistent
electrostatics, finite mean free path, material quantum physics, receiver noise,
loaded DC output or wall-plug power is computed. More search cannot supply
those missing constitutive physics. Next simulation priority is finite bulk
scattering and bias-dependent injection, rather than unlimited optimization of
the same ideal billiard model. No global optimum or practical rectifier claimed.
